A quick napa cabbage kimchi. Ferments are the oldest way to keep a harvest.

## Steps

1. Quarter a napa cabbage, salt it between the leaves, leave 2 hours.
2. Rinse well and squeeze dry.
3. Blend garlic, ginger, a little sugar, fish sauce and gochugaru into a paste.
4. Rub the paste into every leaf, add sliced spring onion and radish.
5. Pack into a jar, press down until liquid covers it.
6. One or two days at room temperature, then the fridge.

> [!warning] Leave headroom
> It bubbles. Leave 3 cm at the top of the jar and open it daily for the first days.
