![[tomato.svg|right|110]] The crop that gets most people into gardening. Homegrown tomatoes taste nothing like shop ones, mostly because they ripen on the plant.

## Growing

1. Sow indoors 6–8 weeks before the last frost.
2. Plant out deep: bury the stem up to the first leaves, it grows roots.
3. Pinch out side shoots on cordon varieties.
4. Water evenly ([[Watering]]); uneven water splits the fruit.
5. Feed weekly once the first truss flowers.

## Problems

> [!danger] Blight
> Brown patches on leaves and stems in warm, wet weather. Remove affected leaves at once, don't compost them, and keep foliage dry.

> [!bug] Blossom end rot
> A black patch at the bottom of the fruit. It's a calcium problem caused by uneven watering, not missing calcium in the soil.
