Saving seed closes the loop: next year's garden comes from this year's.

## Which plants

Save from **open-pollinated** varieties. Seed from F1 hybrids won't grow true to type.

## Tomatoes

1. Squeeze the seeds and jelly of a ripe fruit into a jar.
2. Add a little water and leave it 2–3 days until a mould forms on top. The fermentation removes the germination-inhibiting gel.
3. Rinse, dry on a plate for a week, store cool and dry.

## Beans and peas

Leave a few pods on the plant until they rattle, then shell and dry them.

> [!info] Storage
> Most seed keeps 3–5 years in a cool, dry, dark place. A paper envelope in a jar in the fridge is ideal.
