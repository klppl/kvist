An evergreen note is written to stay useful: it has one idea, a title that states it, and links to related notes. It is revised, not replaced.

Good evergreen notes are

1. **atomic** — one idea per note, so it can be linked precisely;
2. **concept-oriented** — organised by idea, not by source or project;
3. **densely linked** — see [[Linking over filing]];
4. **written for yourself** — but clearly enough for others to follow.

The 🌳 badge on this site marks notes I consider evergreen. Compare a [[Seedlings|seedling]].
