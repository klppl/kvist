Marcus Aurelius, written around 170–180 AD, in Greek, as notes to himself. Never meant for publication, which is what makes it readable.

## Recurring ideas

1. Focus on what is in your control; the rest is weather.
2. Every day you will meet difficult people. Expect it.
3. Everything is change, and you are part of it.

It is effectively a private notebook that was published later: the opposite of [[Learning in public]], and still useful two thousand years on.
