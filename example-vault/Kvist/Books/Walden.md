Henry David Thoreau, 1854. Two years in a cabin by a pond, told as one.

## Notes

- Half of it is an account book: he lists what the cabin cost, down to the nails, to show how little a life needs.
- *The Bean-Field* chapter is about gardening as a way of knowing a place, not as a way of making money. He grows two and a half miles of beans.
- He is at his best describing the pond freezing and thawing.

> [!quote] Thoreau
> I went to the woods because I wished to live deliberately.

Connects to [[Digital gardens]]: a garden as a practice rather than a product.
