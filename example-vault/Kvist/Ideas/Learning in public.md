Share what you learn while you're learning it, mistakes included.

- It forces clarity: you can't publish a vague thought.
- People correct you, which is the fastest way to learn.
- Your notes become a record of how you got here.

The risk is publishing something private by accident, which is why kvist checks every note twice ([[How publishing works]]).
