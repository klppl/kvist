I'm Ada, an imaginary gardener who keeps her notes in Obsidian and publishes some of them here. Nothing on this site is real advice. It exists to show what a [[Digital gardens|digital garden]] made with kvist looks like.

## Why a garden and not a blog

A blog is a stream of finished posts, newest first. A garden is a set of notes that grow: some are [[Seedlings|seedlings]], some are well tended, and they link to each other instead of to dates. Each note shows when it was *planted* and *tended*, and a growth stage:

| Badge | Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 🌱 | seedling | a rough idea, may be wrong |
| 🌿 | budding | taking shape, worth reading |
| 🌳 | evergreen | as good as I can make it |

## This site

- Notes live in one Obsidian vault, synced between laptop and phone.
- A plugin publishes the notes in `Kvist/` and anything tagged `#public`.
- The server builds the site and serves it. See [[How publishing works]].
