Water less often but more deeply: roots follow the water down, and deep roots survive dry weeks.

- Water at the base, not the leaves, to avoid blight on [[Tomatoes]].
- Morning is best; evening water sits on the leaves overnight.
- Mulch keeps the soil moist for days longer.
- Rainwater from the [[Rain barrel]] is free and plants like it better than chlorinated tap water.

A rough rule: vegetables need about 25 mm of water a week. For a 1.2 × 2.4 m bed that is

$$
1.2 \times 2.4 \times 0.025 \,\mathrm{m^3} = 72 \text{ litres per week.}
$$
