Basil grows best next to tomatoes ([[Companion planting]]), and by August there is always too much of it.

- 2 big handfuls of basil leaves
- 1 clove of garlic
- 30 g pine nuts, toasted
- 40 g parmesan, grated
- olive oil, about 100 ml
- a pinch of salt

Pound garlic and salt in a mortar, add the nuts, then the basil a handful at a time, then the cheese, then loosen with oil. A food processor works, but pulse it: heat turns basil dark.

Freeze it in an ice cube tray, without the cheese, to keep summer for winter.
