Some plants grow better together. The evidence ranges from solid to folklore, so treat this as a list of experiments.

| Plant | Good neighbours | Why |
|---|---|---|
| [[Tomatoes|Tomato]] | basil, marigold | basil may deter thrips; marigolds attract hoverflies |
| Beans | squash, corn | beans fix nitrogen, corn gives them a pole |
| Carrot | onion, leek | the smells may confuse carrot fly and onion fly |
| Squash | nasturtium | nasturtiums lure aphids away |

## The three sisters

Corn, beans and squash, planted together the way many Indigenous peoples of the Americas grew them: the corn holds up the beans, the beans feed the soil, and the squash leaves shade out weeds.

Basil from bed 1 ends up as [[Basil pesto|pesto]].
