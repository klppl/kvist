## Previews

Hover any link to another note to see a preview of it. Links to a heading preview just that section: try [[Sourdough#Schedule]] or [[Composting#The recipe]]. On a phone, the first tap shows the preview and the second opens the note.

## Private links

[[Diary]], [[Draft]] and [[Secret corner]] are private. [[Not written yet]] doesn't exist. All four look the same: muted text, no link.

## Backlinks

At the bottom of every note, *Links to this note* lists the notes that link here, with the sentence around each link.
