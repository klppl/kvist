A 200-litre barrel under the shed's downpipe. One summer storm fills it.

## How much rain is that?

The shed roof is 3 m × 2 m. Every millimetre of rain on a square metre is a litre, so a 10 mm shower collects

$$
V = A \cdot h = 6\,\mathrm{m^2} \times 10\,\mathrm{mm} = 60\,\mathrm{L}
$$

minus maybe 20 % lost to splash and evaporation.

- [x] food-grade barrel with lid

> [!caution] Mosquitoes
> Keep the lid shut, or add a mesh: standing water is a nursery.
