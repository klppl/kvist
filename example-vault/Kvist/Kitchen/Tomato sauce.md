The simplest sauce, for a glut of [[Tomatoes]], preferably San Marzano.

1. Halve 2 kg of tomatoes, roast at 200 °C for 40 minutes with garlic and oil.
2. Push through a food mill to remove skins and seeds.
3. Simmer until it coats a spoon, salt to taste.
4. Freeze in portions, or bottle it.
