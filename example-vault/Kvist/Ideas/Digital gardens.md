A digital garden is a personal website made of notes that grow over time, linked by topic rather than ordered by date.

## Garden versus stream

| | Stream (blog, feed) | Garden |
|---|---|---|
| Unit | finished post | note at any stage |
| Order | newest first | by link and topic |
| Updates | rarely, after publishing | constantly |
| Tone | performative | exploratory |

## Why I keep one

- Writing in public makes me finish thoughts ([[Learning in public]]).
- Links surface connections I wouldn't otherwise notice ([[Linking over filing]]).
- Old notes get better instead of going stale ([[Evergreen notes]]).

> [!quote] An old idea
> The commonplace book, where readers copied passages and their thoughts on them, is the same idea several centuries earlier.
