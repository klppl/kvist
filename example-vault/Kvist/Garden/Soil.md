Healthy soil is mostly not dirt: it's air, water, living things and organic matter around a skeleton of minerals.

## Texture

Rub moist soil between your fingers:

| Feels | Mostly | Drains | Holds nutrients |
|---|---|---|---|
| gritty | sand | fast | poorly |
| silky | silt | medium | well |
| sticky | clay | slowly | very well |

Loam, the gardener's favourite, is roughly 40 % sand, 40 % silt and 20 % clay.

## pH

Most vegetables like a pH between 6 and 7. The scale is logarithmic, so soil at pH 5 is ten times more acidic than at pH 6:

$$
[\mathrm{H^+}] = 10^{-\mathrm{pH}}
$$

## Improving it

The answer is nearly always the same: add organic matter. Spread 5 cm of [[Composting|compost]] each autumn and let the worms dig it in. ^add-compost

> [!warning] Don't dig wet clay
> Working clay soil while it's wet squeezes the air out and leaves hard clods for the whole season.
