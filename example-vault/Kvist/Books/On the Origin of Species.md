Charles Darwin, 1859. Surprisingly much of it is about gardeners and breeders: pigeons, cabbages, gooseberries.

- Chapter one starts from *variation under domestication*: what people did to plants by saving seed from the best ones, which is exactly [[Seed saving]].
- The idea of natural selection is introduced by analogy with that.
